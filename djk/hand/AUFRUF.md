## Tools (90)

DJ: `lage` · `bestand` · `laden` · `deck_start` · `deck_stopp` · `sprung` · `loop` · `pad` · `loop_nach_box` · `box` ·
`regler` (ramp over bars; project mixer paths `spur/1..32/*`, `grp/1..16/*`, `pm/fader` are rejected by the server with `ueber_projekt`; 1.15 mix-tool paths `<source>/gate/*` (not erz/8), `<source or bus>/util/*`, `bus/n/mb/*`, `bus/n/lim/*` and `spektrum/kanal`, a wrong channel kind such as `bus/1/gate/an` is a 400 `pfad`; `lim/*` does nothing while `kern.toml` `kanal_limiter_vorhalt_ms` is 0) · `fx` · `fx_zuweisung` · `abbrechen` · `tempo` (decks follow since 2026-10-06: varispeed, then a swap to a version at the new tempo, ADR 028) · `warte` · `hoeren` · `oszilloskop`. In addition, the studio tools
below and the media library (`bibliothek` · `vorbereiten` · `sets` · `set_zeige` · `set_lege` · `set_vorbereiten`) as well as `instrumente` · `modul_blend`. `instrumente {melodie: "fluegel"}` puts a sampled grand piano (Ableton Core Library,
4 velocity layers, Carla SFZ) on MELODY instead of Surge, `"surge"` switches back (2026-10-06).

Decks: `deck` is 1, 2, 3 or 4 for `laden`, `deck_start`, `deck_stopp`, `sprung`, `loop`, `pad` (decks 3 and 4 behave like deck 2; a deck above 4 is refused with `unbekanntes_deck`). Deck loop lengths are beats: `loop` takes 0 (off), 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64; `pad` loop cues take 1 to 64. The hearing check (`hoeren`, hearing certificates) covers all four decks; the sync partner of a deck is the lowest other deck that runs with an open channel (such verdicts stay `validiert: false`).

Live modules (live-module task 4): `live_oeffnen` opens the live container (the persistent project of the set; Cypher cannot confirm a switch away from a running one: `live_laeuft`). `modul_export {deck, slot?}` turns the running loop of a deck (normally deck 3) into a module of slot A or B (without `slot`: the first free slot whose channel is closed, so A, else B; `kanal_offen` naming `pad/1` and `pad/2` when no free slot has a closed channel; a named slot is strict): the loop sounds at once, the stems follow in the background. `modul_stand` shows both slots and the separation state, `modul_stems {slot}` separates again after a failure, `modul_leeren {slot, takte}` fades a module out over 0, 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 bars and frees the slot. A module plays on a DJ channel (DJ view places, ADR 037): slot A (Loop 1) on `pad/1`, slot B (Loop 2) on `pad/2`, through the strip of that channel; the group fader of the module stays at 0 dB and the channel fader is the only gain. The channel is closed when the module arrives and I cannot open it (`regler` on `pad/N` while a module occupies the slot is `kein_hoerschein`); an export, `modul_einsetzen` or a `modul_setzen` through `proj_aendern` into a slot whose channel is open (trim plus fader above -26 dB, or a planned or running ramp of mine on its fader or trim; PFL does not count) is refused with `kanal_offen`, whoever sends it. Known limit: `proj_undo`, `proj_redo` and `proj_oeffnen` can bring a module back into a slot without this check. The original of a stem is the fader of its original track (`spur_aendern`), `modul_setzen` marks a group as a module (repairs). Andreas sees the same on the page: EXPORT next to the loop buttons of decks 1 to 3, the LIVE button in the head of the Loops place, which carries the live container panel.
Loop boxes are live slots (DJ view places, task 11): `loop_nach_box {deck, box}` is `modul_export` into slot A (box 1) or B (box 2), `box {box, aktion: laden, name}` loads a library loop into the slot as a module (`POST /modul/loop`; `kanal_offen`, `slot_belegt`, `loop_unbekannt`, `kein_liveprojekt`), and `box` with `start` or `stopp` answers `box_ersetzt` (a module plays through its channel strip). Every slot argument of the module tools is 1, 2, A or B (1 = A, 2 = B).

Searching the library: `stoebern` pages through the whole media library by type (`track`, `loop`, `oneshot`, `strudel`) with
text search, field filters, Camelot neighbours, BPM and energy filters, sorting and pages (read only). `anschluss` names,
for the running deck (or for a `material_id`), the matching library tracks, ranked by tempo, genre,
Camelot move and energy class, with `punkte`, `zug` and `gruende` per hit (read only).

Sounds (plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-06-klaenge.md`): `klaenge` searches all sounds except tracks (one-shots, loops,
impulse responses, recordings, generated material, voices) in the media library and the own loop library; each hit carries `einsatz`:
`sofort` (plays now, own loops via `box`), `werkstatt` (open file, needs kit build or loading) or `nur_live`
(encrypted Ableton pack sample, never load). `bestand_karte` counts the inventory per type and deployment level, the largest
packs and the loop library. Sound wanted: first `klaenge`, then build.

**`proj_warp {clip_id, von_beat?, bis_beat?, bei_beat?, marker_max?, transienten_max?}`** (package 1.13) reads the warp of an audio clip: `warp` is `null` without warp, otherwise `{an, modus, quelle, eins_unsicher, transpose_st, detune_ct, versatz_beats, marker_zahl, marker_gezeigt, weiter_ab_projekt_beat?, tempo {mittel_bpm, min_bpm, max_bpm}, erster_marker {quell_s, beat}, marker: [{quell_s, beat, projekt_beat, segment_bpm}]}`. The list has at most `marker_max` entries (1 to 4096, default 200; a workshop map has 350 to 2300, one per beat, beat 0 is the first one): `marker_zahl` is the number in the clip, `marker_gezeigt` that of the answer, `weiter_ab_projekt_beat` the first marker not shown (as `von_beat` of the next page); `tempo` and `erster_marker` summarise the warp without the list. `quell_s` is the source second, `beat` the clip beat (0 = bar 1.1.1 of the source), `projekt_beat` the position in the first pass, `segment_bpm` the source tempo of the segment that begins at the marker (`null` at the last marker). `bei_beat` (project beat) additionally returns `bei {projekt_beat, quell_s}`: the source second that plays there, as a cross-check. Changes go through `proj_aendern`: `clip_neu` with `warp: "auto"`, `warp_auto {clip_id}`, `warp_setzen {clip_id, an?, modus?, beats?, transpose_st?, detune_ct?, warp?}` (modus `repitch | beats | complex | tones`; mode `beats` (slice 4): drum units, each starts on its beat and keeps its shape; parameter `beats {raster: transienten | 1/4 | 1/8 | 1/16 | 1/32, loop: aus | vor | vor_zurueck, huelle: 0..100}`, default `transienten`/`vor`/`100`, the whole object is sent; `beats` follows the tempo like `repitch` and rejects `transpose_st`/`detune_ct` ≠ 0 with `transpose_repitch`; `proj_lage` reports `modus: 'beats'`, `proj_warp` the object `beats`), `clip_aendern {versatz_beats}`; the source of the auto warp in this order: workshop map of the file (same sha256, one marker per beat, beat 0 = first one, the clip starts there and reaches to the end of the file), Traktor grid, bars, library BPM; one-shots never warp. If there is no tempo, `proj_aendern` reports `hinweise: [{index, hinweis: "kein_tempo"}]` (`warp_auto` rejects with `kein_tempo`); a long track (from 60 s) without map and without Traktor grid is analysed in the background by the workshop: `hinweise` then names `analyse_angefordert` (if it is already running: `analyse_laeuft`), the clip is switched over on arrival (a history entry `Auto-Warp`), `proj_lage` lists the running analyses under `analyse [{clip_id, status: wartet|laeuft}]`. An unusable map reports `karte_zu_gross` or `karte_unbrauchbar`, the next source applies. `modus` is `repitch` (the pitch follows the tempo), `complex` (whole tracks, the pitch stays) or `tones` (tonal, finer grain); `warp_setzen modus` switches it. `transpose_st` (whole semitones, −12 to 24) and `detune_ct` (cents, −50 to 50) detune the clip in `complex` and `tones`, the tempo stays; together −1200 to 2400 cents (below −12 semitones the attack in the arrangement would come too late). In `repitch` they do not exist (`transpose_repitch`): whoever switches from `complex` to `repitch` sets both to 0 in the same `warp_setzen`. A change takes effect after a short moment (the voice is re-set in the warp engine, about 43 ms plus one cycle) and needs a core from the same state as the page server (an older core does not play the detune). A core without Bungee rejects COMPLEX and TONES on loading (`warp_maschine_fehlt`). Slice 4: `transienten_zahl` (hits of the server in the source within the clip; `null`: audio not yet decoded), `transienten` (source seconds of the hits in the window `von_beat`..`bis_beat`, at most `transienten_max`, default 4096, up to 32768) and `transienten_gezeigt`.

**`proj_auto {lane_id | spur_id, von_beat?, bis_beat?, bei_beat?, punkte_max?}`** (Automation Slices 1 and 2) reads the points of one lane or of all lanes of a track: `{lane_id, spur_id, ziel, gesperrt?, punkte_zahl, punkte_gezeigt, punkte: [{beat, wert, form}], weiter_ab_beat?, bei?: {beat, wert}}`, with `spur_id` `{spur_id, lanes: [...]}`. Beats are project beats. `ziel` and the unit of `wert`: `fader` (dB, -200..6, -200 = off), `pan` (-1..1), `mute` and `solo` (0 or 1, always jumps), `geraet.lautstaerke`, `geraet.cutoff`, `geraet.resonanz`, `geraet.env_mod` (0..1, only on tracks with the RB-338 device (not the bk device)). `gesperrt: true` appears only on a locked lane. `form` is the segment into the point (`linear` | `s` | `sprung`). The query parameter `bei_beat` returns `bei {beat, wert}` (the answer key is `bei`, not `bei_beat`): the value the core plays at that beat, in the unit of the lane (before the first point its value holds, after the last point the last one). `von_beat`/`bis_beat` are inclusive, `punkte_max` defaults to 200. Changing goes through `proj_aendern`: `auto_neu {spur_id, ziel, punkte?: [{beat, wert, form?}]}` (without `punkte` two points at the loop brackets on the current value of the target, with `punkte` the lane starts with these points, checked like `auto_punkte`: this is how a recorded take is written; `lane_doppelt`, `ziel_unbekannt`), `auto_punkte {lane_id, setze?, loesche_von?, loesche_bis?}` (delete first, both ends inclusive, then set; a set point replaces every point at the same beat; `punkte_ungueltig`, `zu_gross`; the last point can only go with `auto_loeschen`), `auto_loeschen {lane_id}`, and `auto_aendern {lane_id, gesperrt: boolean}` (lock or unlock; `auto_punkte` and `auto_loeschen` on a locked lane are refused with `lane_gesperrt`, 409). Switching a track with `geraet.*` lanes to a host device is refused with `geraet_lanes`. Every command is one undo step, `lane_unbekannt` for an unknown id. The track-head value only changes the base value; the line is what plays. `proj_lage` shows `lanes: [{id, ziel, punkte_zahl, gesperrt?}]` per track. `proj_zusammenfuehren` additionally answers `lanes_verworfen` (the mute and solo lanes of the muted sources that the merge dropped; undo restores them).

**Clip envelopes** (Automation Slice 5, 1.12) go through `proj_aendern` and are read with `proj_auto`; no tool is added by this (the tool count is unchanged). `huellkurve_setzen {clip_id, ziel, punkte, verknuepft?, loop_beats?}` sets the envelope of one target on one clip (it replaces the old one, one per target and clip); `huellkurve_loeschen {clip_id, ziel}` removes it (`huellkurve_unbekannt` when there is none). `ziel` is `gain` (audio clips, dB -200..0 on top of `gain_db`), `transpose` (MIDI clips, semitones -48..48, rounded at the start of each note; the notes in the project stay unchanged), or a modulation target that rides RELATIVE on the track's own value or lane: `spur_fader` (dB -60..6), `spur_pan` (-2..2), `geraet.lautstaerke | cutoff | resonanz | env_mod` (-1..1, MIDI clips on RB-338 tracks only). `punkte` are `{beat, wert, form?}` with CLIP beats (from the clip start, rising, at most two at one beat = a jump, 1..8192 points). Linked (default): the points of `[0, clip loop)` repeat with every loop copy of the clip; `verknuepft: false` with `loop_beats` (1/16..clip length): they repeat modulo `loop_beats` from the clip start. Reasons: `huellkurve_ziel` (400, the target does not fit the clip), `punkte_ungueltig`, `zu_gross`, `clip_unbekannt`, `geraet_huellkurven` (a track with `geraet.*` envelopes cannot switch to a host device). There is no lock for envelopes; with Stop Cypher set both commands are refused with `ki_gestoppt` (409) like any project change. `proj_lage` shows `huellkurven: [{ziel, punkte_zahl, verknuepft?, loop_beats?}]` on a clip that has envelopes (without the points). `proj_auto {clip_id, ziel?, von_beat?, bis_beat?, bei_beat?, punkte_max?}` reads them back: `{clip_id, spur_id, ziel, verknuepft, loop_beats?, punkte_zahl, punkte_gezeigt, punkte: [{beat, wert, form}], weiter_ab_beat?, bei?: {beat, wert}}`; without `ziel` `{clip_id, spur_id, huellkurven: [...]}` (an empty list when the clip has none). For a clip envelope ALL beats (the points, `von_beat`, `bis_beat`, `bei_beat`) are CLIP beats; `bei` is the value the core plays at that clip beat, including the repetition of a linked or unlinked envelope (`gain` and `spur_fader` in dB, `transpose` in semitones before rounding). `clip_id` does not go with `lane_id` or `spur_id` (`argument_ungueltig`).

**Lane editor tools** (Automation Slice 4) also go through `proj_aendern`: `auto_vereinfachen {lane_id, von_beat, bis_beat, toleranz?}` (thins the points of the range within the tolerance, default fader 0.3 dB, pan 0.008, `geraet.*` 0.004; only runs of linear segments are thinned, `s` and `sprung` points stay), `auto_dehnen {lane_id, von_beat, bis_beat, neu_bis_beat}` (stretches `(von_beat, bis_beat]` onto `(von_beat, neu_bis_beat]`, the lane after the later end keeps its values), `auto_neigen {lane_id, von_beat, bis_beat, faktor -1..1}` (a point at the share u of the range moves to u^(4^faktor), 0 changes nothing) and `auto_form {lane_id, von_beat, bis_beat, form: rampe_auf | rampe_ab | sinus | dreieck | rechteck, perioden? 1..64, unten?, oben?}` (replaces the points of the range by the shape, a jump at both edges keeps the old value outside; mute and solo take `rechteck` only). Each is one undo step and refused on a locked lane (`lane_gesperrt`); an empty range or one outside 0..100000 is `bereich_leer`, a shape that does not fit is `form_ungueltig`. Read the result with `proj_auto`.

**`proj_bk {aktion: lage | merken | laden, kanaele?}`** (Baukasten S17) is the sound snapshot of the open project: the Baukasten graph on erz/2, erz/3, deck/1..4 (fx/3, fx/4 and erz/5..7, pad/1..2 are accepted by the file format) and the eight graph knobs `<kanal>/bk/1..8`. `lage` compares saved against live per channel (`gleich` true, false, or null when a live value is unknown). `merken` saves what is loaded now as ONE undo step "Baukasten: save sound" (default channels: erz/2, erz/3, deck/1..4 that have a graph; it never removes an entry; knobs whose live value is unknown keep their stored value and are listed in `bk.unbekannt`). `laden` applies it now. Opening the project and a kern restart apply it by themselves (knobs first, then the graph; channels whose stream has AUTO off are skipped for Cypher: `auto_aus`; a missing graph file gives the hint `bk_graph_fehlt`, a changed one `bk_graph_geaendert`). The project command `bk_setzen {kanal, graph | null, regler?, sha256?}` (proj_aendern) edits one entry; `graph: null` removes it. Undo changes only the project, it does not change the running sound.

**`proj_stems {clip_id | spur_id, von_beat?, bis_beat?}`** (package 1.13 slice 6; model and GPU rule since 2026-11-06) splits an audio clip (whole or `von_beat..bis_beat`, at most 600 s source) on the GPU with BS-RoFormer into drums, bass, other and vocals, plus guitar and piano when they are not empty (otherwise their signal is in other). GPU only: less than 4.5 GB free ends with `fehler` `gpu_belegt`, no GPU with `keine_gpu`, the project unchanged. The result is ONE project change with one undo step: the original in the range is muted (`stumm` on the clip, `clip_aendern`), four to six tracks in the group "Stems: <name>". The tool starts (`POST /proj/stems` → `{auftrag_id, frames}`) and polls `GET /proj/aktuell?noten=zahl` until `stems.zustand` is no longer `laeuft` (`fertig | abgebrochen | veraltet | fehler`, with `grund` and `text`) or the deadline (20 min) is over; then it answers `laeuft` with `auftrag_id`, without abort. If the project was switched or closed (the run then aborts) or the state is missing, it answers `unbekannt` with `detail`: check `proj_lage`. `proj_lage` carries the same field `stems` (last state, stays after the end). `proj_stems_abbrechen {auftrag_id}` aborts (`kein_auftrag` for an unknown id; `zu_spaet` only while the run takes over the result, after that `kein_auftrag`; undo takes the stems back).

**`proj_zusammenfuehren {spur_ids, von_beat, bis_beat}`** (Paket 1.13 Slice 7, merge) renders the chosen tracks (2 to 32, distinct) in isolation over the range, with their own faders and pans and the group and the production master neutral, and adds ONE new audio track with the result in one undo step; the sources are muted. The WAV lies at `merge/merge-<id>.wav` in the project folder, is never replaced and stays after an undo. The new track carries +3.01 dB (`PAN_MITTE_DB`) against the pan law. The null test against the solo export of the sources is below -90 dBFS (measured at the target). Answer: `{rev, spur_id, datei, frames, hinweis, quellen}`. Refusals are listed in the table above; the call waits for the render and answers `hand_frist` after the tool limit (`DJK_HAND_FRIST_PROJ_ZUSAMMENFUEHREN_MS`).

**`proj_bounce {art, spur_id?, spur_ids?, gruppe_id?, von_beat?, bis_beat?, ziel_spur_id?, ziel_beat?}`** (package 1.14 slice 4, bounce, N08) posts exactly the given fields to `/proj/bounce?antwort=kurz`. `art: 'an_ort'` bounces an audio track in place: one pre-mixer render (fader 0 dB, pan 0, mute off, without its lanes, dry) over the hull of its clips; the clips move with their ids into a new take lane `Before bounce`, the main lane gets one clip with +3.01 dB clip gain, and fader, pan, sends and lanes stay live. Restore with `proj_aendern` `comp_waehlen` of that lane over its clip hull. `art: 'neue_spur'` is the merge of `proj_zusammenfuehren` with one source allowed, or all tracks of a group (`gruppe_id`); the range defaults to the hull of the sources. `art: 'einfuegen'` renders the sources post-fader over `von_beat..bis_beat` onto the audio track `ziel_spur_id` at `ziel_beat`, overwriting its main lane there; the sources stay unchanged. One undo step each; the WAV lies at `bounce/bounce-<id>.wav` and stays after an undo. Refusals as tool errors, among them `bounce_nur_audio`, `bounce_ziel_art`, `takes_voll`, `ersetzen_loop`, `gruppe_unbekannt` and the merge codes; the call waits up to 80 min (`DJK_HAND_FRIST_PROJ_BOUNCE_MS`), then answers `hand_frist`. Interface: `docs/architecture/INTERFACES.md` section 20.7.

**`proj_midi_import {pfad, vorschau?, auswahl?, zuordnung?, tempo?, profil?, label?, rev?}`** (MIDI import, spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-midi-import.md`) reads a Standard MIDI file (format 0 or 1; SMPTE time base and format 2 are refused with `smf_smpte` and `smf_format2`) that the page server can read at `pfad` (absolute, `.mid`, `.midi` or `.rmi`, at most 8 MiB) and plans ONE command list (`tempo_setzen`, `spur_neu`, `spur_aendern` for a muted track, `clip_neu` with the notes) that runs through the same path as `proj_aendern`: all or nothing, one history step, so one `proj_undo` takes the whole import back. Positions are exact (`tick / ticks per quarter` beats; a clip starts on the whole beat before its first note). The report names every part by its key `<MIDI track>.<channel 1..16>`; `auswahl` and `zuordnung` use these keys. Defaults: the drum channel 10 plays the kit `gm` (built from the kit `techno` when the kit folder has none), every other part the RB-338, which plays one note at a time (the report gives `polyphonie` per part and the hint `midi_mono`); `profil: poly` moves chord parts to Baukasten graphs (at most 8). The tempo of the file becomes the project tempo when the project holds no track yet (`tempo` overrides); tempo changes inside the file and a time signature other than 4/4 are NOT mapped and are reported as hints (`midi_tempowechsel`, `midi_taktart`). More parts than free tracks is `midi_zu_viele_spuren` (nothing is created; select fewer with `auswahl`). `vorschau: true` answers the same report without writing.

**`proj_resample {strang_id, punkt?, von_beat?, bis_beat?}`** (1.10 slice 7, spec 5.7) posts exactly the given fields to `/proj/resample` and answers `{rev, spur_id, clip_id, datei, frames, quelle, punkt, von_beat, bis_beat}` (no project). `strang_id` is `s<n>`, `g<n>`, `r<n>` or `master`; the core plays the strip once in real time (`/k/arr/abgriff`, at most 256 beats, the loop range or the whole song by default), the server turns the file into `resample/resample-<id>.wav` and puts it on a new track "<source> (resampled)" with `gain_db` +3.01 for the pan law: below a source track, at the end otherwise, source unchanged, ONE undo step "Resample <name>" (the WAV stays after undo). `punkt` `post` (default) is what the strip sends to its target, `pre` its input before the chain; the monitor of an armed track is part of `post` only. A silent strip is `resample_leer` (no track, no file), a dead Surge host for a sounding Surge track `wirt_fehlt` or `wirt_fehler`; the other reasons are those of `proj_freeze` plus `resample_fehler`. Waits like `proj_freeze` (6 min, then `hand_frist`).

**Markers by hand** (package 1.13 slice 3b) via `proj_aendern`; beats are clip beats (the beats of the markers, 0 = bar 1.1.1 of the source), each command one undo step, `quelle` of the warp becomes `hand`: `warp_tempo {clip_id, faktor: 2 | 0.5}` (beats of all markers, offset, clip length and loop times factor; `warp_faktor` outside 15..800 BPM), `warp_marker {clip_id, setze?: [{quell_s, beat?}], loesche?: [beat], verschiebe?: [{beat, neu_quell_s | neu_beat}]}` (delete, move, set; without `beat` a new marker gets the beat of the grid there; reasons `marker_unbekannt`, `warp_marker`, `warp_faktor`), `warp_ab_hier {clip_id, beat, art: gerade | start}` (Warp From Here; `start` sets the auto warp anew from there, `kein_tempo` without source), `eins_hier {clip_id, quell_s}` (Set 1.1.1 Here; the clip begins at the new one, the offset stays, so the track moves in the project by the beats that the spot lay behind the old one (as in Live), its length follows; if the track is in the deck's inventory and this server writes corrections, it goes as a line `eins` `von andreas` (also when Cypher sends, spot rounded to the next whole map beat) into `korrekturen.jsonl`: the next auto warp of the track begins there, the deck after its next version; undo takes the clip back, not the line; `hinweise`: `eins_gemeldet`, `eins_nur_hier`, `eins_rueckweg_fehler`), `tempo_vom_clip {clip_id}` (project tempo = the tempo at which the clip sounds as long as the piece of the source that it plays; like `tempo_setzen`: `deck_laeuft`, `tempo_bereich`). Quantize audio: `audio_quantisieren {clip_id, raster: 1/4 | 1/8 | 1/16 | 1/32, staerke? 0..1 = 1, von_beat?, bis_beat?}` (`von_beat`/`bis_beat` are clip beats, the beats of the markers, 0 = bar 1.1.1 of the source, not project beats as with `proj_warp`; default: the range of the clip, with a loop the first copy; the grid applies in the project) sets a hand marker on the grid at each transient of the source (in the range) (strength 1 exactly on it, 0.5 halfway); markers outside stay, inside they are replaced; the length stays; one undo step. Rejections: `transienten_fehlen` (source not yet decoded), `keine_transienten`, `quantisieren_konflikt` (the new markers would not be strictly increasing), `warp_faktor` / `warp_marker` (limits 15..800 BPM, 4096 markers; never silently truncated). Read back with `proj_warp`.

**`proj_lautheit {von_beat?, bis_beat?}`** measures the loudness per track and as a sum, offline and only on request (no measurement runs in the core). The server renders each track that plays something in the range
individually (fader and pan of the track from the project, then through its group and the production master, all other tracks muted (key sources keep running inaudibly: a compressor on this track's path that is keyed by another track, group or return still ducks), without the mixer state of the core; the tap is the production master, before the set limiter) and once all together, then ffmpeg measures each WAV. Range as with `proj_export`
(default loop bracket, 1 to 1024 beats). Surge tracks with notes are frozen beforehand (the Surge host of each slot, arr1 or arr2, is needed). Nothing is written to the project or to the export folder.
Answer: `{von_beat, bis_beat, spuren: [{spur_id, nr, name, lufs_i, lra, true_peak_dbtp, spitze_db, stumm, unter_messgrenze}], summe: {lufs_i, lra, true_peak_dbtp, spitze_db, unter_messgrenze, regler_quelle}}`. `lufs_i` is the integrated loudness,
with it one matches an 808 kick to a bass (the peak is misleading there). A muted track, one with its fader off, or one silent in the range reports `stumm: true` and `lufs_i: null` (not −70); if any track of the project is on solo, a track without solo counts as silent, as in the mix. A quiet but not silent track below the measurement floor
of −70 LUFS (peak between −90 and about −68 dBFS) reports `unter_messgrenze: true`, `stumm: false`, `lufs_i: null` (not −70.0) and `lra: null`, the true peak remains measured. Tracks without content in the
range are missing. The sum matches `proj_export` of the same range (default `abgriff: produktion`) to 0.1 LU (`regler_quelle: projekt` for the sum, `kern` for the export). `spitze_db` is the sample peak at the production master, before the set limiter.
Rejection as with `proj_export`, plus `wirt_fehlt`. The deadline of 80 min covers normal ranges; in the edge case (up to 33 renders (32 tracks and sum) of 1024 beats at 60 BPM, each at the time limit) the server allows about 39.3 h, then `hand_frist` comes.

**The utility stage (arr-fx; `proj_aendern`)**: `insert_setzen {strang_id, pos, art: "utility", werte?: {breite, mono_unter, gain, balance}}` puts a stereo tool on a track, a group, a return or the master (a pool of eight in the project, no latency). `breite` 0..4 is the stereo width in mid/side (1 = unchanged, 0 = mono, 2 = side doubled, 4 = side x4 = +12 dB; the mono sum keeps its level, but a width above 2 raises the side part by up to 12 dB and the peak can rise: there is no clipping guard), `mono_unter` 0 or 1..500 Hz sums everything below that frequency to mono (0 = off; 1 to 19 act as 20 Hz, a value between 0 and 1 is refused; a jump of `mono_unter` can be briefly audible, the step rings out in about 2 ms, so lanes on `mono_unter` are not recommended), `gain` -60..24 dB, `balance` -1..1. `insert_param_setzen {insert_id, param, wert}` moves one value. The same pools since arr-fx slice 2: 12 compressors, 8 EQs, 4 saturators, 2 limiters, 8 utilities. The pools are a ceiling, not an operating case: placed stages cost, a full pool exceeds the cost gate (`ins_voll` is the refusal when a pool is used up).

**The effect stage and the delay return (arr-bk; `proj_aendern`)**: `insert_setzen {strang_id, pos, art: "bk", graph, werte?: {k1..k8}}` puts a Baukasten EFFECT graph on a track, a group, a return or the master (a pool of eight in the project). `graph` is the file name of the graph in the graph folder; the `graphen` tool lists them, and only the ones with `art: "effekt"` qualify (an unknown name or an instrument graph is `argument_ungueltig` with the reason in the text, a ninth stage is `ins_voll`). `k1..k8` are 0..1 (default 0.5), what they do is the `regler_namen` of the graph; they move with `insert_param_setzen` and can be driven by a lane (`auto_neu` target `insert.<stage id>.k1`). `insert_setzen {..., ersetzen: true, graph: <other>}` swaps the graph on the same slot (the core crossfades over one block, no click); the latency of the graph (at most 128 samples) is compensated like every stage. The shipped standard case is `fx-delay-sync`: a tempo-synchronous stereo ping-pong delay (dotted eighth, wet only; knob 1 feedback, knob 2 level). A delay return in one go: `proj_aendern {befehle: [{befehl: "return_neu", name: "Delay", effekt: {graph: "fx-delay-sync"}}]}` (the Hall is off, the chain carries the stage; `return_neu` without `effekt` stays the Hall return), then `send_setzen {strang_id: <track id>, return_id: <return id from the result>, db: -6}` turns a track up into it. The delay time follows the project tempo (the same beat the transport plays), also in the export.

## Description to sound (1.9 O28)

Two commands of `proj_aendern` and the search tool `presets_suchen`. All of them go through the project, so each is one undo step and `proj_undo` restores the render bit for bit.

- `geraet_richtung {spur_id, wort, staerke?}`: a word of the closed preset vocabulary moves the RB-338 parameters of the track by bounded steps (0.12 x staerke, staerke 1..3, default 1, clamped to 0..1; project values are the core's 0..1). Table: `bright` (cutoff +, env_mod +), `dark` (cutoff -, env_mod -), `aggressive` (resonanz +, accent +), `soft` (resonanz -, accent -), `tight` (decay -; filter envelope only: on the RB-338 a slightly shorter bright tail, the note keeps its length), `deep` (cutoff - by 2.5 steps). Name the call `<word> <track name>`. Refusals: `richtung_unbekannt` (404, the detail lists the words of the device; audio, bk and kit tracks have none; `deep` does not exist on Surge), `richtung_grenze` (409, every parameter of the word is already at its bound, nothing changes), `argument_ungueltig` (staerke outside 1..3).
- On a Surge track (`geraet: {art: wirt, name: surge}`) the same call moves entries of the sparse parameter map `geraet.parameter` (`{name: value}`, display units of `surge_bereiche.json`, at most 32 entries, set with `spur_neu` or `spur_aendern`; `spur_aendern` takes the whole `geraet`). Words: `bright`/`dark` (`a_filter1_cutoff` +/-), `aggressive`/`soft` (`a_filter1_resonance` +/-), `tight` (`a_env1_release` and `a_env1_decay` down); step sizes per `staerke` in `djk/ui/project/direction.ts` (`SURGE_RICHTUNGEN`), clamped to the range, `richtung_grenze` when nothing moves. The word starts from the value in the map or, when the map lacks the name, from the value the host plays now; no running host: `wirt_wert_unbekannt` (409), nothing changes. Filter 1 off in the patch the Surge host plays (`a_filter1_type` 0, as in the factory Templates/Init Saw): `bright`, `dark`, `aggressive` and `soft` move only filter 1 and are refused `richtung_wirkungslos` (409, the text names the word), project, rev and host unchanged, because they would not change the sound; `tight` moves the envelope and still works; the filter is never switched on for you (a type change reloads the state and clicks), load a patch with filter 1 on or use another word. The map is sent to the Surge host (`fahre`) when the project opens, on every change, undo and redo, and before a freeze; a name leaving the map goes back to the host value from before the first send in this session. The Surge host's own patch stays the base: only the named parameters belong to the project. A name or value outside the range: `argument_unbekannt` / `argument_ungueltig`. `geraet.patch` (optional, 1..128 characters, a name of `klang liste`: an own sound or a factory patch like `Pads/MKS-70 Warm Pad`, own sounds win) makes the track play that sound: the project stores the name, the server loads the file into the host of the slot (`lade`) when the project opens, when `patch` changes (command, undo, redo) and before a freeze, export or loudness, and sends the parameter map on top after every load; without `patch` the host stays untouched (old projects). Set it with `spur_neu` or with `spur_aendern` and the whole `geraet` (a changed patch is one undo step and drops the freeze); an unknown name is `patch_unbekannt` (404), a Surge rack member takes no patch. `GET /proj/surge_patches?text=` lists the library for the page's picker.
- `presets_suchen`, then `preset_anwenden {spur_id, preset}`: `preset` is an id from the search (`werk:rb338/dark-dub`). The preset's values (display units) replace the whole parameter object of the RB-338 track (the inverse of `Def::wert` in `rb_dsp`; a parameter the preset does not name goes back to the plugin default). Refusals: `preset_unbekannt` (404), `argument_ungueltig` (malformed id, not an RB-338 track).

**Sidechain keys of a compressor stage (1.10 slice 5; `proj_aendern`)**: `insert_setzen {..., art: "komp", sc?, key?}`, `insert_param_setzen {param: "sc" | "key", wert}` and `duck_durch_kick {spur_id, kick_id, pos?}`.
`sc: n` is the input of track `n` before its chain (the 4a key, a control). `key: {quelle: "spur"|"grp"|"ret"|"deck"|"erz", nr, punkt: "pre"|"post"}` names every other source (`spur` + `post` = the output of a track after its fader and mute; `deck` 1..4 and `erz` 1 are the DJ mixer inputs and need no `punkt`); `{quelle: "spur", nr, punkt: "pre"}` is stored as `sc: nr`. A stage has one of the two. Refusals (400): `sidechain_spaet` (the source is computed after the stage), `sidechain_zyklus` (the key depends on the stage itself), `sidechain_quelle` (the source is not available in the arrangement block). The rules are the data file `docs/interfaces/schluessel_regeln.json`. A DJ key is live only: `proj_export` and `proj_lautheit` have no deck input, and the export answer carries `dj_schluessel: true`. `duck_durch_kick` adds a compressor with the Kick Sidechain values keyed by the kick track in one undo step.

## Building a track

Command sequence with the tools; the commands run in the project, the answers of `proj_aendern` carry only `rev`, `kern` and `noten_zahl`, the
track IDs (`s1`, `s2` …) are assigned by the server, which is why `proj_lage` reads them before the clips. A command in the list that fails makes the whole list
ineffective (all or nothing, one undo step). Prerequisites: no deck plays (otherwise `tempo_setzen` gives 409 `deck_laeuft`), Stop Cypher is not set
(`ki_gestoppt`), and no transport runs that Andreas started or took over after Stop Cypher (otherwise `proj_neu` gives 409 `transport_laeuft`).

1. `proj_neu {"name": "Mini"}` creates the project and opens it (slug `mini`).
2. `proj_aendern` with `label: "Tracks"` and the commands `tempo_setzen`, three times `spur_neu`:

```json
[{"befehl":"tempo_setzen","bpm":128},
 {"befehl":"spur_neu","art":"audio","name":"Kick"},
 {"befehl":"spur_neu","art":"instrument","name":"Bass","geraet":{"art":"intern","name":"rb338"}},
 {"befehl":"spur_neu","art":"instrument","name":"Lead","geraet":{"art":"wirt","name":"surge","slot":1}}]
```

3. `proj_lage` returns `spuren[].id` and `nr` (here `s1` Kick, `s2` Bass, `s3` Lead; do not guess, read).
4. `proj_aendern` with `label: "Clips"`: `clip_neu` with notes, `spur_aendern`, `loop_setzen`. Audio only with a `sha256` from the library (`klaenge`
   searches one-shots, `einsatz` must be `sofort`; the kick below is the 808 one-shot of the acceptance track from 20.10.):

```json
[{"befehl":"clip_neu","spur_id":"s1","art":"audio","start_beat":0,"laenge_beats":8,"loop_beats":1,
  "sha256":"67526f9fd62b0f340fd5bb9d63e34db2832b37d2bbc5067e05e9ab29022886c9"},
 {"befehl":"clip_neu","spur_id":"s2","art":"midi","start_beat":0,"laenge_beats":8,"noten":[
   {"beat":0,"laenge":0.5,"ton":33,"vel":100},{"beat":1,"laenge":0.5,"ton":45,"vel":100},
   {"beat":2,"laenge":0.5,"ton":33,"vel":100},{"beat":3,"laenge":0.5,"ton":45,"vel":100}]},
 {"befehl":"clip_neu","spur_id":"s3","art":"midi","start_beat":0,"laenge_beats":8,"noten":[
   {"beat":0,"laenge":7.5,"ton":69,"vel":80}]},
 {"befehl":"spur_aendern","spur_id":"s2","fader_db":-4},
 {"befehl":"spur_aendern","spur_id":"s3","fader_db":-10,"pan":0.25},
 {"befehl":"loop_setzen","an":true,"von_beat":0,"bis_beat":8}]
```

   `beat` of a note counts within the clip, `loop_beats: 1` makes the kick strike anew on every beat (clip loop up to `laenge_beats`), `ton` is the MIDI number (33 = A1).
5. `proj_transport {"aktion":"start","beat":0}` plays the loop (quantised to 2 if a deck, a loop box or a Strudel stream runs, otherwise immediately); listen with
   `pegel` or through Andreas. To carry on: `proj_transport {"aktion":"stopp"}`: freeze and export need a standing transport.
6. `proj_freeze {"spur_id":"s3","an":true}` freezes the Surge track (real-time run). The step is optional before the export: `proj_export` freezes
   unfrozen Surge tracks with notes by itself.
7. `proj_export {}` writes the loop range (here beat 0 to 8) as WAV to `~/cypherdj-projekte/mini/export/` and reports `datei`, `frames`, `spitze_db`,
   `lufs_i`, `lra`, `true_peak_dbtp`.

**Notes from a Strudel pattern** (strudel-clip): instead of writing notes by hand, `proj_muster {spur_id, start_beat, code, takte?}` (or the command `clip_aus_muster` inside `proj_aendern`) turns a Strudel pattern into an ordinary MIDI clip. 1 cycle = 1 bar = 4 beats. On a kit track (`geraet.kit`) `s("bd*4, ~ hh ~ hh")` resolves the sounds against the kit (`bd`, `bd:2`; an unknown name is refused with the list of the kit's sounds); on an RB-338, Baukasten or Surge track `note("gs1 ~ gs1 b1").velocity("<0.8 1>")` or `n("0 2 4").scale("C:minor")`. `gain` and `velocity` become the note velocity; `degradeBy`, `sometimes` and `?` are frozen into the notes. `takte` absent: the clip is as long as the pattern takes to repeat (16 bars if it never does); a pattern that repeats sooner than the clip sets the clip loop. Controls with a target become clip envelopes that ride relative on the track (`cutoff`/`lpf` to `geraet.cutoff` and `resonance`/`lpq` to `geraet.resonanz` on an RB-338, `pan` to `spur_pan`, a gain driven by a signal to `spur_fader`); everything without a target (`room`, `delay`, `speed` ...) is not applied and is listed as `ohne_ziel` on the clip (`muster.ohne_ziel`) and in the answer. The pattern stays on the clip as `muster.code` (`proj_lage`); `clip_aus_muster {clip_id}` generates the clip again from it (notes and envelopes replaced, id, place and length kept). `clip_aus_muster {clip_id}` on a clip whose notes or envelopes were changed by hand since the pattern made them is refused `handarbeit_vorhanden`; `ueberschreiben: true` replaces them. `cpm` is ignored (the tempo is the project's) and listed in `ohne_ziel`. A pattern that does not run is refused `muster_fehler` with the line and column, nothing is created; a pattern with more than 20000 events or that needs too much memory is refused `muster_zu_gross`. The mapping table is the data file `djk/ui/project/muster_zuordnung.json`.

**Instrument rack** (1.9; `geraet: {art: 'rack', kette, glieder: [{id, geraet, ton?, vel?, kette?, gain_db?}]}` in `spur_neu` or `spur_aendern`): one track plays up to 4 members (ids `g1`..`g4`) at once. A member is an RB-338, a bk graph, a kit or a Surge slot (no rack inside a rack; a Surge member has no `parameter`; Surge members take production slots). Each member has optional zones as `[lo, hi]` pairs (a missing zone = the whole range): `ton` 0..127, `vel` 1..127 and `kette` 0..127, and `gain_db` -60..+12. The rack's own `kette` (0..127) is the chain value; a member sounds while that value lies inside its `kette` zone. At most 8 racks in a project. A rack with a Surge member needs a freeze before export. `spur_aendern geraet: {art: 'rack'}` turns the current device into member `g1`. Commands: `rack_glied_setzen {spur_id, glied}` (add or replace by id), `rack_glied_entfernen {spur_id, glied_id}`, `rack_kette {spur_id, kette}` (the chain value of the rack); each is one undo step. Refusals: `keine_rackspur`, `rack_voll_glieder`, `glied_unbekannt`, `rack_letztes_glied`, `slot_belegt`. Macros reach members too: `rack_setzen` takes a second target kind `{glied, param, von, bis}` next to `{insert, param, von, bis}` (a member id `g1`..`g4`; RB-338 `lautstaerke`, `cutoff`, `resonanz`, `env_mod` and bk `k1`..`k8` with `von`/`bis` 0..1, `gain_db` of any internal member -60..+12; a Surge member is refused with `makro_ziel_wirt`, a target on a track without a rack with `glied_ohne_rack`). Variations of the four macro values: `makro_variation_speichern {strang_id, name}` (at most 8 per strip: `variation_voll`), `makro_variation_laden {strang_id, name}` (a live change), `makro_variation_loeschen {strang_id, name}` (`variation_unbekannt`). A mapping change keeps the stored variations; removing the last mapping removes the rack and its variations (one undo brings them back).
`geraet: {"art":"intern","name":"bk","graph":"juno-pad","parameter":{"k1":0.7}}`. `graph` is the name of an instrument graph from `graphen` (an effect graph or
an unknown name is refused with `argument_ungueltig`), `parameter` holds the knobs `k1` to `k8` (each 0..1, absent = 0.5). The notes of the track play through that graph
inside the core, live and in `proj_export`, `proj_lautheit` and `proj_zusammenfuehren`, with no freeze (`proj_freeze` on such a track is refused with

At most 8 bk tracks per project. The graph file is read when the playback loads; a missing or broken file refuses the playback with `format`.

**Recording in four lines** (1.8 slice 1, internal stream onto an audio track):

1. `proj_aendern` with `spur_aendern {spur_id, arm: true, eingang: {art: "intern", name: "erz/1"}}` (erz/1 is the Strudel channel).
2. `proj_aufnahme {"aktion":"start","von_beat":0}` arms the core, starts the transport and records from its start.
3. `warte` or music via `strudel`; the take is the internal stream, bit-identical.
4. `proj_aufnahme {"aktion":"stopp"}` stops the transport, writes `aufnahmen/take-<kennung>-<spur>.wav` and places one clip per track (one undo step); `proj_lage` shows the clip.
   If the transport ends otherwise (`proj_transport`, hand button, end of the arrangement), the server saves the take itself; `stopp` then delivers the result once, after that it is under `letzte` in `proj_aufnahme`.

**Count-in, punch, overdub** (1.8 T5): the settings are project data, set with `proj_aendern` `aufnahme_setzen {vorzaehler_takte: 0|1|2|4, punch: {ein, aus, von_beat, bis_beat}, overdub}` (at least one field; `punch` fields can be given singly, but `bis_beat` must stay greater than `von_beat` against the stored value, so send both when you move the range). With `vorzaehler_takte` above 0 the transport counts in (clicks) and `proj_aufnahme start` answers only once the take has begun; `einsatz_ausgeblieben` (409) means it did not begin in time and nothing was recorded. With `punch.ein` and/or `punch.aus` on, the stop result carries `punch {von_beat, bis_beat}` per take (the kept range; the raw file stays whole); `punch_leer` means the range does not touch the take and nothing was placed. `overdub` matters for MIDI takes only (adds notes instead of replacing the range); an audio take always replaces the range. I never start a recording into Andreas' running transport: that is refused with `transport_laeuft`; when Andreas does it himself the take begins at the next bar (`laufend: true`, count-in ignored), and stopping it ends only the recording, never his transport.

**One call for a Baukasten instrument** (S15, stream bounce): `proj_strom_aufnehmen {spur: "Bounce", strom: 3, graph: "juno-pad", code: "note(\"c3 e3 g3 c4\")", takte: 4}` does the five calls above in one go (it creates the track if the name does not exist, arms it on `erz/3`, loads the graph, plays the pattern, records four bars, stops, disarms the track again) and stores `entstehung` (graph, the eight knob values, code, tempo) on the clip. ## Pre-listening

`hoeren` measures a channel behind its fader (Ohr (ear), slice 1): six bands, loudness, peak, overlap with the
running master per band, level difference, EQ suggestion (low/mid/high in dB) and a bass swap hint. Only meaningful on a **closed** deck
(the measurement tap lies before the fader, ADR 008): `laden` → `deck_start` (fader stays
closed) → `warte 4` (phrases need a full 4-bar window) → `hoeren` → `regler` (EQ/trim, closed allowed) →
`warte 4` → `hoeren` again (the measurement sits after the EQ).

`vergleich.trim_vorschlag_db` is the **absolute** target value for `deck/N/trim` in dB (old trim minus level difference, clamped to ±24;
the measurement tap lies after the trim, ADR 008), valid only on a closed deck. `trim_unbekannt: true` instead of
the suggestion: the page does not know the old trim (after its restart); also none if a page is silent.

Since slice 3, the page server issues from this measurement a listening pass per bar (`urteil` in the `hoeren` answer
and in `lage.decks[].hoerschein`) and sends it to the core when `urteil: 'ok'`. `regler` on `deck/1`/`deck/2`, which
opens the channel (trim + fader above −26 dB), then automatically appends `hoerschein: hs_id`. No separate call
needed, only wait until `hoeren` shows `urteil: 'ok'` (`sync_ms`/`deck_gegen_deck_ms` are included only as the reason
`sync_unvalidiert:<ms>`, without effect on the verdict, Rev. 4). Without a valid certificate it stays at 409 `kein_hoerschein`,
now with `urteil` and `gruende` of the last certificate in the body.

## Automation tracks (Studio S6, 2026-09-29)

A track is a JSON file with rides on the bar grid, counted from the next bar (`--ab takt`, default)
or phrase one (`--ab phrase`). Examples in `docs/demos/spuren/`.

    djk/tools/djk-spur [--instanz f] show      # running tracks, with rejected parts

### Loop (1.12 S7, 2026-11-04)

`spur` with `schleife_takte` n (1..64) plays the moves as a pass of n bars again and again until `spur_stopp`; pass k starts n bars after
pass k−1, with no gap. Every move lies inside the pass: `ab_takt` ≤ n, a ramp ends at bar n+1 at the latest, no set on bar n+1 (the next pass
starts with its own set there), no `strom:` targets (400 `spur`). Change a running loop of mine with `spur` and `aendern: true` (same name,
`schleife_takte` required, it may change): it applies from the next pass, never inside the current one; the answer gives `ab_beat`/`ab_takt`,
`uebernaechster: true` when the next pass was already on its way. `aendern` exists for loops only (409 `keine_schleife`) and only for the owner
(403 `nur_andreas` on his loops). My loop ends by itself when Andreas grabs one of its targets, when AUTO goes off on a stream it moves, when a
pass would open a closed channel (D8, also at the seam between two passes) or on Stop Cypher. `spuren` shows `schleife_takte`, `durchlauf`, the
running pass (`anker_beat`, `ende_beat`) and `naechste` (a change waits). Interface: INTERFACES §20.6.

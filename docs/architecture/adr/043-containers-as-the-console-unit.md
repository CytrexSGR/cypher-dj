## Context

The core knew channels, not containers. Fourteen source channels fed the mixer: `deck/1` to `deck/4`, `erz/1` to `erz/8`, `pad/1`, `pad/2`. A container (a place of the DJ view) was a redirect on top of finished strips. Four things followed. The streams 9 and 10, the loop boxes and the sends of the source strips ran past the container. There were two mixers and four graph hosts. The identity of a channel existed in many copies (name lists in the core, the page, the hand, the controller maps). And a module in the arrangement engine reached its container only through a fixed channel number (`pad/1` or `pad/2`, later up to six), so Strudel could not sit in every container.

The foundation was sound: the real-time callback, the one clock, the satellites, the pointer swap with fades and the compiler of the building block graphs. The structure above it was not.

## Decision

1. **A container is a channel strip.** There are four containers A to D. Their strip paths are `deck/1`, `deck/2`, `pad/1`, `pad/2` (the existing identifiers stay). Each has exactly one strip (trim, EQ and kill, filter, graph insert, fader, sends 1 to 4, PFL, crossfader side, target master or `bus/1` to `bus/4`) and exactly one input. Nothing reaches the master past a container. The mixer channels `erz/1` to `erz/8`, `deck/3` and `deck/4` as channels and the place channel (`platz_kanal` with its content codes) are gone.
2. **One input per container, chosen by `/k/container/quelle`.** The input is `leer`, `player` (the source buffer of deck player N, container N plays player N) or `modul` (the DJ output of a top group of the arrangement engine). A change blends over 480 samples. A container without a module sleeps. The command replaces `/k/platz/kanal`; the event `/e/container/quelle` replaces `/e/platz/kanal`.
3. **Source buffers with one writer each.** Streams 1 to 16, deck players 1 to 4 and the inputs `ein_1..4` each write their own buffer; the return path of a host goes into the buffer of the stream whose MIDI target the host serves. There is no `+=` into shared mixer inputs any more. `Kern::audio` runs in three stages: `quellen_block` (sources fill their buffers), the arrangement block (the module level computes), then the mixer (the console). The former silent ordering rule is gone, and a test pins the stage order.
4. **Modules are subgraphs of the arrangement engine with one output: the input of their container.** A module is a top group with a DJ output (`ausgang` `dj` `nr` 1 to 4 = container A to D). A container carries at most one module, and any module may sit in any container, a Strudel module in A or B included. No second graph machine is built next to the engine.
5. **The Strudel module is a group with `strom` tracks.** It holds one track of the `art` `strom` per active stream, 1 to 16. The streams 9 and 10 are ordinary streams of that group, the capacity does not fall. A `strom` track reads the source buffer of its stream; rendering stays in the generator, with kit, `bk:` graph or MIDI to a host, so the Strudel semantics are untouched. Each track has the track strip of the engine (inserts, DJ filter, three-band isolator with kill, fader, pan, mute, PFL, trim) and sends into the module returns Hall and Delay.
6. **Aliases keep the old paths.** `erz/<n>/<suffix>` (n in 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10; suffixes fader, trim, pfl, eq, kill, filter, komp, pan, mute, solo, send/1..4, bk/<k>) are resolved in the page server onto the `strom` track n of the Strudel module (`djk/ui/stream_alias.ts`). The alias is not a core address. Controller maps, the hand and scripts run unchanged. `strom/<n>/bk/<k>` carry the Baukasten knobs of a stream in the core.
7. **Project and playback formats.** `cypherdj-projekt/3` adds `plaetze` (four entries with the type per place: `leer`, `track`, `strudel`, `seq`, `loops`, `stems`), `Gruppe.strudel`, `strom` tracks and `Return.strudel`; `/2` and `/1` are lifted in memory, the former browser layout becomes `plaetze` once, afterwards the page reads only the project. `cypherdj-arr/4` adds the track source `strom`, `ausgang` `dj` with `nr` 1 to 4 meaning the containers (the former "N + 2" numbering and the numbers 5 and 6 are gone) and `returns` with `nr` 1 to 8. The loader refuses a live project with `live_ohne_container` (a sounding track or return without a container owner, or a track straight on the production master), `dj_doppelt` (two top groups on one container), and a track that sends out of its module is `send_ausser_modul` (project model). A refusal carries a text to the page and the hand.
8. **Production is a service.** The one arrangement instance and the one open project stay. In a live project every sounding track lies under a container group. In the production project the production master (PM) feeds the service "production": a hidden strip without a mixer channel and without a DJ strip, straight to the master. The PM tap stays the export, and the render stays bit-equal (the old `erz/4` level and export do not change). The previewer (listening only to cue) is a service of the console as well. `erz/4` is no mixer channel; the name stays reserved (`reserviert`).
9. **The send mask is derived from the DJ numbers.** A module track sends only into returns of its own module, and those returns end in the container. Only container strips send into the console returns `fx/1` to `fx/4`.
10. **Eight returns.** The arrangement engine has eight returns (`ARR_RETURNS`, the limit rose from 4 to 8). The Hall pool computes all eight in parallel, one worker per return except return 1 (`hall_faeden` default 7). The Hall pool, the graph pool and the track pool stay separate.
11. **Hearing license and operating rules sit at the container.** The license is keyed on `deck/<player>` (a player in a container) and on `pad/1` and `pad/2`, as before. The silence rule on a module change and ADR 023 I1 to I4 stay operating rules, now at the container instead of the channel kind. Nothing becomes stricter than before; Strudel in A or B is allowed.
12. **What was removed.** The `erz/4` mixer rows and the production control rows on a mixer channel, the loop boxes (`/k/loop/laden`, `/k/loop/start`, `/k/loop/stopp`, `/k/loop/raster`, `/zustand/box`, `/e/loop`), the fixed wiring of the streams 9 and 10 to `pad/1` and `pad/2`, the sends of source strips, the pad-specific meaning of the recording codes 21 and 22 (they keep their numbers as taps at the container inputs C and D, codes 23 and 24 tap A and B, see `/k/rec/spur`), the field `kanal` of `/erz/strom` and the generator option `--kanal`. The name lists are generated from one source, `djk/contract/pult.json`, for the core headers, the TypeScript module, the page module and the hand description. A checker, `contract/check_name_lists.py`, compares them.

## Consequences

- **One truth per question.** Place occupation: the live project (`plaetze`). Names: the console table `pult.json` with its generated modules. Invariants: the loader of the core.
- **Bit-equal renders.** The production render is compared old against new (null render); a console oracle records the old mixer against the new one on fixed cases, including the FX sends.
- **A fifth container is a table entry**, not a rebuild. Not done: a freely patchable console, a JIT, a shared worker pool, new DSP, splitting up `Arrangement::mischen`.
- **The doubled graph host stays** (graph host in the generator beside the engine); it is material for a later round.
- **Containers are not latency-matched among each other**, so the hand delays nothing. Inside a module the group delay compensation stays (ADR 037 item 9); host instruments compensate by a note lead.
- **Cost.** The hall of eight returns computes in parallel and needs four more worker threads; its load is measured in the studio check. Stored projects of format `/2` are lifted on read; a live project written as `/3` is not readable by an older build.
- **Open.** The studio check by Cypher and the listening acceptance by Andreas; the hand and the skill `cypher-dj-live` follow the container picture outside this repository.
